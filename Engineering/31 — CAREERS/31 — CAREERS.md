## For each role, ask

1. **What do they actually do all day?** Usually less modelling and more plumbing than expected.
2. **What's the portfolio evidence?** A deployed system beats a certificate ([[Certification map]]).
3. **Which project here proves it?** See [[28 — PROJECTS]].

## The honest advice

> **Depth in one full stack beats shallow familiarity with ten tools.** A finished [[Capstone build guide]] with tests, CI and a live URL is stronger evidence than any list of technologies.
