**Overview:**

- This app will gamify the way that students in college throw waste away
- This will motivate students to not litter which will therefore make the college campus a cleaner place
- By making the college a cleaner place this will make future students want to come and study at soilhull college. It will also make the environment for eco friendly

  

**how will this app work?**

1. The app will require a student to currently be at a bin location
2. Once at a bin location the 1 minute timer will start
3. Within the duration of the 1 minute the student will have to do 4 things
4. Scan the QR code on the bin they are currently at
5. scan the barcode of the packaging they wish to throw away
6. take a picture of the waste they have in hand
7. input what the item is and the material
8. once all 4 steps are complete the student is rewarded with green points
9. green points are points that students that can turn into rewards which the college offer. These can be vouchers for costa coffee thats inside the college as an example
