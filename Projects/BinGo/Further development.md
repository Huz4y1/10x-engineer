  

- There will be a map of all bin locations throughout the campus
- With all the pictures of waste data collected this will allow us to label each image and preprocess the data so we can use deep learning to train a machine learning model to tell the student which bin it will go in. This makes the environment even more greener as we are implementing recycling and not just purely throwing waste in the bin

  

new flow

1. The student will be directed to a bin location
2. once confirmed that they are at a bin the 1 minute timer will start
3. Within the 1 minute the student will have to do 3 things
4. scan the barcode of the item they wish to throw away
5. take a picture of the waste they want to dispose
6. the custom built deep learning model will tell the user which bin to put the waste in
7. the student will have to scan the QR code of the bin type they have been told to put the waste in
8. The student will be granted with green points which they can convert into real college rewards
