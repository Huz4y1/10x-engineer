  

# **The ML Engine (the real moat)**

This is what makes your idea powerful.

You’re predicting fuel prices using:

**1. Core Data**

- station prices (time series)
- location + fuel type

👉 From UK sources like government fuel APIs

**2. Macro Drivers**

- oil prices (e.g. Brent Crude Oil)
- exchange rates (GBP/USD)

👉 Fuel prices follow oil with a delay

**3. Local Competition**

- nearby stations
- brand (Shell plc, BP, etc.)
- distance between stations

👉 Creates price wars & undercutting

**4. Demand Signals**

- traffic (via Google Maps)
- time of day / week
- holidays
- weather

👉 More demand = higher prices

**5. Key ML Concept: Lag Effect**

- Oil price changes today
- Fuel price changes days later

👉 Your model learns delays like:

- oil_t-1, oil_t-3, oil_t-7
